About

Made by 209.

CCIX is 209 in Roman numerals — and that's where it comes from. It's the in-house extension and hair care line from 209 Salon & Extensions Studio in North Texas.

Our stylists install and maintain extensions every day across five studios. CCIX brings the hair, care and tools they rely on to everyone: clients who want to care for their extensions at home, and salon professionals who want a line they can trust.

What we stand for

  • Real hair. 100% Remy human hair with the cuticle aligned root to tip.
  • Honest shades. Solid, rooted and balayage shades built to blend.
  • Pro first. Methods and tools chosen by working extension artists.
